# Palette's Journal

## 2025-05-14 - Mobile Experience and Accessibility Foundations
**Learning:** Mobile users benefit significantly from specific `inputmode` attributes and larger touch targets. Basic accessibility (using `<label>` instead of `<span>`) is often overlooked in "dev tools" but is crucial for inclusive design.
**Action:** Always check for `inputmode` on numeric inputs and ensure interactive elements meet minimum touch target sizes (at least 44x44px where possible).
